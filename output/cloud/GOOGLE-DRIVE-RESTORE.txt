Restore the playlist archive

Google Drive holds the ZIP as 10 parts because the complete ZIP exceeds the connector's 512 MiB upload limit.

1. Download all 10 ZIP parts (001 through 010), checksums.json, and restore_archive.py into one folder. Keep their original filenames.
2. Open a terminal in that folder. With Python 3.12 or newer, run:

   python restore_archive.py checksums.json --output playlist-archive.zip --extract extracted

The script uses only Python's standard library. The extracted destination must be new or empty. It verifies every part, the reconstructed ZIP, ZIP CRC checks, and the archived source files' SHA-256 checksums before reporting success.
